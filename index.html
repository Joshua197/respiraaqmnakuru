RESPIRA · Egerton University · Nakuru

Air Quality Sense-Making Workshop

Four days bringing together ward ambassadors, students, the Kenya Meteorological Department, county and city officials, health practitioners, traders, planners, media and partners — moving from shared questions, through data and lived experience, to a Nakuru air quality charter.

Notes for participants

  • Days 2 and 3 are smaller sessions. Days 1 and 4 are open to all stakeholders; the secretariat will confirm who is needed on the middle two days.
  • Day 1 runs partly off campus. Transport to the KMD offices is coordinated from Egerton after lunch, departing in time for a 14:30 start.
  • No technical background is needed. The introductions on Day 1 and the story circles on Day 2 draw on your own experience — what you notice at work, at home and in your neighbourhood. You do not need to know anything about air-quality data to take part.
